## 1. The one-liner

> **Cohort Portal — Megz's courses, in one place. Walk into the exam already knowing it.**

## 4. What it actually does (all shipped features)

### 📹 Lectures & content
- **VOD lectures** — watch anytime, **resumes exactly where you stopped**
- **Chapter markers** — jump straight to the topic you're stuck on
- **Solved problem sets & LeetCode-style solutions**
- **Lab walk-throughs**
- **Reading lessons** with proper code formatting and one-tap copy
- **Course documents / notes** in a built-in reader

### ✍️ Practice & feedback
- **Assignments** — upload a file *or* write code directly in the browser
- **Real grading by Megz** — pass/fail plus written feedback, not an auto-marker
- **Quizzes** — MCQ, true/false, and short answer for self-checking

### 🔓 Structure that paces you
- **Sequential unlocking** — pass the week's assignment to open the next one, so you can't leave it all to exam week
- **Progress tracking** — % complete per course, lessons left, what's next
- **Announcements** — course-wide or global, straight to the dashboard and your email

### 💸 Flexible pricing
- **Buy the whole course**, or **buy a single week** on its own
- **Upgrade credit** — if you bought weeks first and then want the full course, what you already paid comes off the price
- **Sales & discounts** shown up front
- **In-person students get 40% off** with their registration code
- **Pay by InstaPay** — send the transfer, upload the screenshot, Megz confirms

### 🎓 For in-person students
- **Registration codes** — your account is linked to the class roster
- **Attendance-based release** — lectures unlock as you attend
- **Lecture VODs included free**; labs and extra material are the paid add-on, at the discounted rate

### 🆓 Try before you pay
- **Free preview lessons — no account needed at all.** Just open and watch
- **Public course catalog** with full outlines, so you see exactly what's inside
- **"Coming soon" courses** so you know what's next

### 🔒 Your account
- Email verification, single active session
- **Save/bookmark courses** for later
- Purchase history with status on every payment

## 8. Visual kit

Pull from `DESIGN.md` so posts match the product.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Ink (text/bg) | `#151313` |
| Flame (CTA) | `#FF5734` |
| Sunny | `#FCCC42` |
| Lilac | `#BE94F5` |
| Sky | `#73C2FB` |
| Mint | `#7FD9A6` |
| Paper (canvas) | `#F7F7F5` |

**Font:** Kodchasan (free on Google Fonts) — bold + tight for headlines.

**Look:** paper background, one saturated card colour per slide, **2px solid black border on every
box**, fully-rounded pills, chunky 22px card corners. Rotate sunny → lilac → sky → mint across
slides so a carousel reads as a set. Keep the flame orange for the CTA slide only — it should mean
"tap this".
